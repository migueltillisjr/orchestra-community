Why P.R.A.Y. exists

An admission into a world of possibilities.

Our mission is to empower underprivileged youth with opportunities they might not otherwise have and a safe space where they can thrive.

Through positive role modeling, teamwork, community service, healthy activities, and meaningful relationships, P.R.A.Y. works to help young people grow into strong, compassionate, and confident adults.

“We want our youth to know there are people in their community who see them, believe in them, and are ready to help them grow.”

— Inspired by P.R.A.Y.'s mission and vision

The future of P.R.A.Y.

A safe place to belong — beyond school and away from the streets.

P.R.A.Y.'s long-term vision is to create a dedicated place where youth can connect, socialize, find support, and participate in family-centered activities — including support for families connected to the recovery community.

Healthy relationships built today can shape a young person's confidence, choices, and opportunities for years to come.
