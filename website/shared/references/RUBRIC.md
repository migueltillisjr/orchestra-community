# Rubric

| Section | Not Evident | Approaching Competence | Competent |
|---------|-------------|------------------------|-----------|
| **A. Purpose and Impact** | The first viewport does not communicate the organization's purpose or who it serves. | The first viewport communicates purpose or audience, but lacks clarity about why it matters or what visitors can do next. | The first viewport clearly communicates what the organization is, who it serves, why it matters, and what visitors can do next. |
| **B. Content Quality and Trust** | Copy contains placeholders, fabricated claims, or unverified information. | Copy is mostly accurate but contains minor gaps, unexplained jargon, or contact information that is incomplete or hard to verify. | Copy is accurate, specific, concise, human, and free of unsupported claims; contact information is easy to verify. |
| **C. Responsive Behavior** | The site is not usable below 768px or displays horizontal scrolling, overlapping controls, or clipped content on mobile. | The site works on mobile but has inconsistent behavior, minor text fitting issues, or unintended layout shifts across breakpoints. | The site works seamlessly at 320px, 375px, 768px, 1024px, and 1440px; all text fits, controls are usable, and no horizontal scrolling occurs. |
| **D. Accessibility and Inclusion** | The site lacks semantic structure, keyboard navigation is broken or missing, or color contrast is inadequate (below WCAG AA). | The site has basic semantic structure and keyboard navigation, but focus indicators are weak, some alt text is missing, or reduced-motion support is incomplete. | The site has semantic structure, logical keyboard navigation with visible focus, proper contrast, meaningful alt text, and respects reduced-motion preferences. |
| **E. Visual Hierarchy and Art Direction** | Typography, spacing, and color lack coherence; decorative elements distract from content or the site appears generic. | Typography and spacing establish a reading path, but visual hierarchy is inconsistent across sections or imagery does not support meaning. | Typography, spacing, contrast, imagery, and color work together to create a clear reading path and distinctive point of view. |
| **F. Interaction and Usability** | Navigation is confusing, links are broken or non-functional, and error or empty states are absent or misleading. | Navigation works but is inconsistent; some links or buttons behave unexpectedly; feedback on user actions is incomplete. | Navigation is clear and consistent; all interactive elements respond predictably; forms, errors, and success states provide clear feedback. |
| **G. Performance and Technical Quality** | The page loads slowly, assets are oversized, or console errors block functionality. | The page loads acceptably but has some unnecessary dependencies or images that could be optimized; minor console warnings present. | The page loads quickly; assets are appropriately sized; no critical console errors; the site remains stable on slower devices and networks. |
| **H. Content Structure and Findability** | Information is disorganized; headings are unclear or missing; key resources are difficult to locate. | Information is grouped logically but headings could be more scannable; some internal links are generic or the information architecture requires exploration. | Information is well-organized with scannable headings; internal links are meaningful and descriptive; key resources are easy to find. |
| **I. Asset Integrity and Technical Delivery** | Images, fonts, or scripts fail to load; broken requests appear in console. | Most assets load correctly but one or two resources show broken paths or slow loading. | All images, fonts, icons, and scripts load from valid paths without broken requests or avoidable console errors. |
| **J. Honest Content and Delivery** | The site contains placeholder copy, unverified claims, or misleading imagery. | The site is mostly finalized but contains minor placeholder elements or unresolved formatting. | No placeholder copy, fabricated claims, unexplained jargon, or misleading imagery remains in the delivery. |
