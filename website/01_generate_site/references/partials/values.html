These simple principles guide how staff, volunteers, sponsors, and families care for one another and the youth we serve.

01

Kindness

Lead with a friendly, patient, loving attitude and create an environment where young people feel respected and welcomed.

02

Service

Give your best to the people around you. Service builds character, strengthens community, and reminds youth that they can make a difference.

03

Purpose

Remember the bigger mission: bring hope, show love, and help young people move toward healthy possibilities for their future.
