# Name

Info Pioneer
Business process improvement and practical technology consulting

## Positioning

Something in your business is creating unnecessary pain. We identify the operational and technology issues behind that pain and helps you fix them with practical, lasting systems.

Info Pioneer helps find work that is repetitive, messy, or confusing, and make it easier to manage. We help save time, stay organized, and get better results from the tools they already use or equip you with the tools to make your life easier.

## Location

United States, remote-first

## Ideal Client Profile

- Small to mid-sized businesses that already have steady revenue and can invest in operational improvement.
- Teams or sole proprietors that are growing and starting to feel pain from inconsistent processes, duplicated work, or knowledge being trapped with a few people.
- Organizations that want faster execution without adding unnecessary software or complexity.
- Founder-led businesses, operations teams, consultancies, and service businesses that depend on smooth internal coordination.

## Core Problems Solved

- Repetitive administrative work that wastes time and attention.
- Important processes that live only in someone's head instead of in documentation.
- Teams using tools inconsistently or paying for software they are not fully leveraging.
- Poor handoffs, unclear ownership, and recurring confusion about how work should get done.
- Growth that creates operational strain faster than the team can organize around it.

## Services

- Assess current workflows, tools, and team handoffs to identify inefficiencies and bottlenecks.
- Recommend practical ways to improve how the business uses its existing technology stack.
- Design repeatable processes for recurring operational tasks.
- Build internal knowledge bases, SOPs, and documentation tailored to the client's actual use cases.
- Train teams so they can maintain and update their systems without constant outside support.
- Prioritize solutions that create a competitive edge while keeping information private and secure.

## Engagement Process

- Discovery: understand business goals, current tools, pain points, and team workflows.
- Audit: review how work currently moves through the business and where friction shows up.
- Recommendations: identify the highest-value process, documentation, or tooling improvements.
- Implementation: build or refine workflows, documentation, and internal systems.
- Training and handoff: help the team use the system effectively and maintain it over time.

## Deliverables

- Workflow assessments and improvement recommendations.
- Process maps and written SOPs for recurring tasks.
- Internal knowledge base structures and starter documentation.
- Tool usage guidance tailored to the existing stack.
- Training materials or working sessions for the team.
- Prioritized action plan for future operational improvements.

## Outcomes

- Less time spent on repetitive work and preventable back-and-forth.
- Faster onboarding for new team members.
- More consistent execution across the team.
- Better visibility into how work gets done and where issues arise.
- Stronger operational foundation for growth.

## Example Use Cases

- A consulting business that needs clearer internal delivery processes.
- A growing small business that has too many manual handoffs and repeated questions.
- A team that uses multiple software tools but lacks a consistent operating system.
- An organization that wants a maintainable internal knowledge base instead of scattered notes and tribal knowledge.

## Service Packages

- Workflow Audit: focused review of current processes, tools, and bottlenecks with clear recommendations.
- Documentation Buildout: creation of SOPs, playbooks, and internal knowledge base content.
- Process Improvement Project: redesign of a specific workflow from diagnosis through implementation.
- Ongoing Advisory Support: monthly support for maintenance, refinement, and operational guidance.

## Prices

- Project-based engagements from $3,000 to $20,000, sized to stay within an easy approval range for many businesses.
- Optional ongoing support or management retainers for continued optimization, documentation, and process maintenance.
- Pricing depends on workflow complexity, number of teams involved, and the level of implementation support required.

## Timeline

- Small audits can be completed in about 1 to 2 weeks.
- Documentation and workflow improvement projects may take 2 to 6 weeks depending on scope.
- Ongoing support can be structured as a monthly retainer.

## Differentiators

- Focuses on practical execution instead of abstract strategy.
- Improves the value of tools the client already uses before recommending new ones.
- Builds systems the client can understand, maintain, and update internally.
- Emphasizes privacy, clarity, and sustainable process design.

## Constraints

- Not focused on adding unnecessary tools or creating bloated process overhead.
- Best fit for businesses that are ready to act on operational recommendations.
