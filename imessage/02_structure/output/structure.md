## SMS Campaign Structure

### Header
- Brand Name: Skate2Wealth
- Purpose: Financial education, investment strategies, wealth-building tools

### Body
- Introduction: Brief, engaging introduction that aligns with the brand tone and audience expectations.
- Main Content: Clear, concise, and actionable information or offer. Use bullet points or numbered lists for easy readability.
- Call-to-Action (CTA): A clear, compelling CTA that prompts the desired action (e.g., 'Download our free ETF guide now!').

### Footer
- Opt-Out Information: Include a clear opt-out instruction (e.g., 'Reply STOP to opt out').
- Additional Information: Provide any additional necessary information, such as the brand's website or contact details.

### Example Structure
```
Skate2Wealth: Build wealth through smart investing. Download our free ETF guide now! Reply STOP to opt out.
```
