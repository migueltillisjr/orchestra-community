# SMS Messaging Campaign Rubric

| Section | Not Evident | Approaching Competence | Competent |
|---------|-------------|------------------------|-----------|
| **A. Message Strategy & Goal Clarity** | No clear objective or strategy for the SMS campaign is defined. | The campaign has a general objective but lacks specific, measurable goals or clear target audience segmentation. | The campaign has clearly defined, measurable goals and is tailored to specific audience segments with a strategic purpose. |
| **A1. Audience Targeting & Personalization** | Messages are generic with no personalization or audience consideration. | Messages show minimal personalization; audience segmentation is incomplete or inconsistent. | Messages are personalized with relevant audience data; segmentation is strategic and consistent throughout the campaign. |
| **A2. Content Effectiveness & Clarity** | Message content is unclear, irrelevant, or fails to communicate the main offer/CTA. | Message content communicates the offer but lacks clarity, compelling language, or strong value proposition. | Message content is clear, compelling, and effectively communicates value; recipients understand the offer and next steps. |
| **B. Call-to-Action (CTA) & Engagement** | No clear CTA is present; messages do not prompt action or engagement. | CTA is present but weak, unclear, or inconsistent; engagement potential is limited. | CTA is clear, compelling, and strategically placed; messages effectively prompt desired action (clicks, purchases, replies). |
| **C. Compliance & Legal Standards** | Messages do not include required opt-in/opt-out information or violate SMS regulations (TCPA, GDPR). | Messages partially comply with regulations; some required disclosures or opt-out mechanisms are missing or unclear. | Messages fully comply with SMS regulations; proper opt-in/opt-out mechanisms, disclosures, and privacy standards are included. |
| **D. Tone, Voice & Brand Alignment** | Messages are off-brand, unprofessional, or inconsistent with business voice and values. | Messages mostly align with brand voice but show occasional inconsistencies or lack personality. | Messages reflect brand voice consistently; professional tone is appropriate for audience; messaging builds brand trust and recognition. |
| **E. Length, Frequency & Timing** | Messages are excessively long, poorly timed, or sent at inappropriate frequencies causing unsubscribes. | Message length is acceptable but sometimes verbose; frequency may be slightly excessive or timing inconsistent. | Messages are concise (optimal SMS length); sending frequency is appropriate and timing maximizes engagement without fatigue. |
| **F. Response Handling & Customer Service** | No mechanism for handling customer responses or inquiries; messages appear one-directional. | Response handling exists but is inconsistent, slow, or fails to address customer needs adequately. | Responses to customer inquiries are timely, helpful, and professional; conversation flow encourages ongoing engagement. |
| **G. Performance Metrics & Results** | No tracking, metrics, or data provided; campaign effectiveness cannot be measured. | Campaign has some metrics tracked but data is incomplete; ROI or engagement rates are unclear or suboptimal. | Key performance indicators are tracked and analyzed (open rates, click-through rates, conversions); campaign shows measurable success and positive ROI. |
| **H. Professional Communication** | Messages contain numerous errors in grammar, spelling, or punctuation; unprofessional language used. | Messages have minor grammar/spelling issues or slight tone inconsistencies; mostly professional. | Messages are error-free with professional language, proper grammar, spelling, and punctuation throughout the campaign. |
