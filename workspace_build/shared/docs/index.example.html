Hook · Story · Offer

The problem usually is not effort. It is operating drag.

When a business grows, the old way of working starts to crack. Work that used to be simple becomes scattered across inboxes, meetings, spreadsheets, notes, and half-used software. The team keeps moving, but more energy leaks into rework, repeated questions, and unclear ownership.
