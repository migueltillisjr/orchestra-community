Create a professional slide presentation about **[topic]** for **[target audience]**.

The goal of the presentation is to **[inform / persuade / teach / sell / summarize / inspire]**.

Follow these steps:

1. **Clarify the purpose**

   * Explain the main message of the presentation.
   * Identify what the audience should understand, believe, or do after viewing it.

2. **Define the audience**

   * Describe who the presentation is for.
   * Adjust the tone, examples, and word choice to fit that audience.

3. **Create a strong title**

   * Make the title clear, interesting, and relevant.
   * Avoid generic titles.

4. **Build a logical slide structure**
   Include:

   * Title slide
   * Problem or background slide
   * Key idea or solution slide
   * Supporting points
   * Examples, data, or visuals
   * Benefits or impact
   * Call to action or conclusion

5. **Keep each slide focused**

   * Use one main idea per slide.
   * Avoid large paragraphs.
   * Use short bullets, clear headings, and strong visual hierarchy.

6. **Make the presentation visually engaging**

   * Suggest relevant images, icons, diagrams, charts, or layouts.
   * Use consistent colors, fonts, and spacing.
   * Make it look modern, clean, and professional.

7. **Write speaker notes**

   * Add short speaker notes for each slide.
   * Make the notes sound natural, confident, and easy to present.

8. **End with impact**

   * Create a strong final slide.
   * Summarize the main point.
   * Include a clear next step, recommendation, or call to action.

Output the presentation as a slide-by-slide outline with:

* Slide number
* Slide title
* Main slide content
* Suggested visual
* Speaker notes
