New Protocol

Three controls working together.

🔐

Multi-Factor Authentication

Requires something you know, something you have, and something you are (Ubagaram & Pushpakumar, 2021).

🧪

Regular Security Audits

Quarterly reviews, vulnerability assessments, penetration tests, and access reviews.

🎓

Employee Training

Training on phishing detection, password hygiene, and incident reporting (SANS Institute, 2024).

These practices help employees become the first line of defense while giving IT a repeatable process for reducing risk.
