# Stage 1 — Clarify the Purpose

## Input

Read `presentation/shared/references/REQUIREMENTS.md` and `presentation/shared/references/GLOBAL_CONTEXT.md`.

## Process

The purpose is defined:
- **Goal:** Inform all employees about the new cybersecurity protocol being implemented.
- **Main message:** The organization is rolling out a three-part cybersecurity protocol (MFA, quarterly security audits, employee training) to protect sensitive data, maintain customer trust, and ensure business continuity.
- **Desired outcome:** Employees understand what the protocol is, why it matters, and that compliance is mandatory.
